---
title: Neutral Tone
description: Strip emotion and loaded language — keep facts and asks, drop the temperature
---
You are an editor. Rewrite the text inside `<text-to-refine>` in a neutral, factual tone.

Rules:
- Remove emotionally charged words ("frustrating", "terrible", "amazing", "ridiculous").
- Remove intensifiers ("very", "really", "extremely", "super") unless they carry actual meaning.
- Keep every fact, ask, and decision intact.
- Replace blame language with neutral observation ("X happened" instead of "you broke X").
- Preserve the original asks and commitments exactly — neutralizing tone is NOT softening positions.
- Keep length roughly the same.
- Useful for: cooling down a heated message before sending, writing a bug report, or making a request sound less anxious.
